---
title: "General FAQ"
items:
  - q: "What is Consultant Client Ops?"
    a: "A site of templates, an ebook, and free tools built specifically for freelancers and independent consultants to run client engagements professionally — proposals, contracts, scope, invoicing, and pricing — without building every document from scratch."
  - q: "Are the templates legal documents?"
    a: "The Freelance Service Agreement Template is a structured starting point covering commonly-included clauses, not legal advice. We recommend having a lawyer review any contract template before use, especially for high-value engagements, and adapting it to your jurisdiction."
  - q: "What format do the templates come in?"
    a: "Each template in the Consultant Client Ops Kit comes in two formats: a polished, print-ready PDF and an editable markdown file that imports natively into Notion (Import → Text & Markdown) and Google Docs — so you can work in whichever tool you already use. A START-HERE guide in the download walks through it."
  - q: "Do I need the whole Kit, or can I buy templates individually?"
    a: "The Consultant Client Ops Kit is sold as one bundle of all 12 documents — this keeps the price low per document and ensures the documents that reference each other (like the SOW and the Change Order) stay consistent."
  - q: "Is there a subscription or is this a one-time purchase?"
    a: "One-time purchase. You buy the Kit or the ebook once and keep access to the files — no recurring fee."

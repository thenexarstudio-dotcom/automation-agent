---
title: "Freelance Contract Template FAQ"
items:
  - q: "Is the Freelance Service Agreement Template legally binding as-is?"
    a: "It's a well-structured starting point covering commonly-needed clauses (payment terms, IP ownership, confidentiality, termination, liability), but it isn't tailored to your jurisdiction or situation — have a lawyer review it before use in higher-value or higher-risk engagements."
  - q: "Does the contract template cover international clients?"
    a: "It includes a governing law and jurisdiction clause you fill in yourself, but contract law varies significantly by country and state — get local legal review for cross-border engagements."
  - q: "How does this relate to the Scope of Work Template?"
    a: "The contract references the Scope of Work as an exhibit, so deliverables live in one document (the SOW) and the legal terms live in another (the contract) — update the SOW without needing to redraft the whole agreement."

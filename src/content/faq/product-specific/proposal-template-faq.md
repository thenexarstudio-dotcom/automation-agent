---
title: "Consulting Proposal Template FAQ"
items:
  - q: "Should I include pricing directly in the proposal?"
    a: "Yes — proposals without a price tend to stall, because the client can't act on them without a follow-up conversation. Include the investment and payment terms directly in the document."
  - q: "How customizable is the proposal template?"
    a: "It's a structure (situation, approach, deliverables, timeline, investment, exclusions, next step), not fixed copy — every section is meant to be rewritten in your own voice and specific to each client."
  - q: "Does this work for retainer proposals, not just project-based ones?"
    a: "Yes — swap the 'investment' section for a monthly retainer fee and scope-per-month, and keep the same overall structure."
---

## What Bonsai is built for

Bonsai (and similar tools like HoneyBook and Dubsado) are full client-management platforms: proposals, contracts, invoicing, and scheduling in one connected software subscription, billed monthly or annually.

## When that's the right choice

If you want everything connected in one system — automated invoice reminders, integrated scheduling, e-signature built in — a subscription platform is genuinely the better fit, and worth the recurring cost.

## When a template kit makes more sense

If what you actually want is well-structured *documents* — a proposal, a contract, a scope of work, an invoice — that you can send from tools you already use (Google Docs, Notion, your existing invoicing method) without adding another monthly bill, a one-time template kit does that job at a fraction of the ongoing cost.

## Feature comparison

| | Bonsai / HoneyBook / Dubsado | Consultant Client Ops Kit |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | Monthly or annual subscription | One-time payment |
| What you get | Integrated software platform | 12 ready-to-use documents |
| Where you use it | Inside their platform | Any tool you already use (Notion, Docs, Sheets, PDF) |
| Automation (reminders, e-signature, scheduling) | Yes, built in | No — pair with your existing tools |
| Best for | Freelancers who want one connected system | Freelancers who want strong documents without a new subscription |

## The honest recommendation

If you're already comfortable with your invoicing and scheduling tools and just need the documents themselves to be sharper, the [Consultant Client Ops Kit](/templates/consulting-proposal-template) is the lower-cost, lower-commitment option. If you want full workflow automation across scheduling, e-signature, and payments in one login, a subscription platform is worth evaluating on its own merits.

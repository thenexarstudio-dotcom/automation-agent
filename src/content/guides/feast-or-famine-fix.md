## The cycle this book fixes

Slow stretch. Panic. A burst of outreach. A wave of new clients. Fully booked. No time left for outreach. Pipeline runs dry. Repeat.

It feels like a client problem. It's a systems problem — and it comes down to three fixable things: pricing that doesn't match your real capacity, proposals that don't close reliably, and no pipeline cadence that runs independent of how busy you currently are.

## What's inside

- **Chapter 1 — Pricing:** building a real day rate from your actual numbers, and why a tiered rate card outperforms a single hourly number.
- **Chapter 2 — Proposals:** the seven-section structure that gets proposals signed, and a follow-up cadence that recovers deals that would otherwise go quiet.
- **Chapter 3 — Scope:** why scope creep is a documentation problem, and how a specific SOW plus same-day change orders protect the deal you already closed.
- **Chapter 4 — Pipeline:** a fixed weekly cadence of outreach, visible expertise, and referral asks that runs regardless of current workload — so the gap between projects stops happening.
- **Chapter 5 — The weekly system:** how the four habits fit together into a repeatable operating rhythm.

## Who this is for

Freelancers and independent consultants who are good at the work but feel like new business only happens in emergencies — and want a specific, repeatable system instead of one more piece of general advice to "network more."

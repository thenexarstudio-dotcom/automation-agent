Most freelancers set their rate by guessing, copying a competitor, or picking a round number that feels reasonable. None of those are grounded in what you actually need to earn or how much time you actually have to bill. This calculator uses three numbers you already know — your income target, your realistic billable days, and your overhead — to produce a day rate, an hourly equivalent, and a suggested price for a project of a given length.

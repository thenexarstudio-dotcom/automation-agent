## Why invoices get paid late — and it's rarely about the client

The most common reason a freelance invoice sits unpaid for weeks isn't that the client is avoiding you — it's that the invoice is missing information accounts payable needs to process it (a PO number, a clear due date, an itemized breakdown), so it sits in a queue until someone chases it down. A well-formatted invoice removes that friction entirely.

## What every invoice needs

### Header information
Your business name, address, and contact info; the client's billing contact and company name; a unique invoice number (sequential, e.g. INV-0012 — needed for both your records and the client's AP system); the issue date and due date stated explicitly (not just "Net 30" — the actual calendar date).

### Reference numbers
A PO number if the client's process requires one (ask during onboarding, via the Client Onboarding Questionnaire) and a reference to the contract/SOW this invoice bills against. Missing PO numbers are one of the single most common reasons invoices stall in enterprise AP systems.

### Itemized line items
Each deliverable or milestone billed as its own line — matching the language used in the Scope of Work — with quantity/rate/amount. Avoid a single vague line like "consulting services"; itemized invoices are processed faster and disputed less.

### Payment terms
Due date, accepted payment methods, and the late fee percentage that applies past the due date — stated on the invoice itself, not just buried in the contract, so it's the last thing the payer sees before approving.

### Total and currency
Subtotal, any tax if applicable to your situation/jurisdiction, and a clearly bolded total — in the currency agreed in the contract.

## A habit that matters more than the template

Send the invoice the same day a milestone is delivered or approved, not at the end of the month in a batch. The single biggest lever on how fast you get paid is how promptly you ask — a same-day invoice, paired with the format above, resolves most of what causes freelance payment delays.

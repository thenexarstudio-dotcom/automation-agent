## 7A. Cancellation and Kill Fee

If Client cancels this engagement prior to completion of all deliverables described in the Scope of Work, the following applies in addition to Section 7 (Termination):

**Notice required:** Client will provide [Your Name / Business Name] with no less than **[14 / 30]** days' written notice of cancellation.

**Structure (select one):**

- [ ] **Percentage of remaining fee.** Client will pay a cancellation fee equal to **[10–25]%** of the remaining (unbilled) contract value as of the date of cancellation, in addition to payment for all work completed to date.
- [ ] **Work-to-date plus flat cancellation fee.** Client will pay for all work completed to date, plus a flat cancellation fee of **$[Amount]**, adjusted as follows based on notice given: [full fee if notice < 14 days / 50% if 14–30 days / waived if 30+ days].

## Cancellation Fee Calculation

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total project fee | $[Amount] |
| Work completed to date | $[Amount] |
| Remaining (unbilled) contract value | $[Amount] |
| Kill fee (per structure selected above) | $[Amount] |
| **Total due upon cancellation** | **$[Amount]** |

## Payment Timing

The kill fee is due alongside the final invoice for work completed to date, payable within **[X]** days of the cancellation notice, per the payment terms in the Service Agreement.

## Notes

- This clause supplements, and does not replace, the standard "payment for work completed" language already in the Service Agreement's Termination section.
- Consider adding this clause to retainer agreements as well, where early cancellation can end recurring income with little warning.
- Revisit the percentage or flat amount for engagements of unusual size — a single fixed number rarely fits every project.

# Late Payment Reminder Email Templates

**How to use:** Send Email 1 the day the invoice becomes overdue, Email 2 around 7-10 days overdue if unpaid, and Email 3 (final notice) around 20-30 days overdue if still unpaid. Replace every [bracket] with specifics, and adjust the day counts to match your own contract's terms.

---

## Email 1 — Polite reminder (day of / day after due date)

> Subject: Quick nudge — Invoice [#INV-0001] due [Date]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Hope you're well! Just a quick note that Invoice [#INV-0001] for $[Amount] was due on [Date] and I don't see payment come through yet — this happens, so just flagging it in case it slipped by.
>
> You can pay via [payment method/link]. Let me know if you need me to resend the invoice or if anything's holding it up.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Email 2 — Firm follow-up (~7-10 days overdue)

> Subject: Following up — Invoice [#INV-0001], now [X] days overdue
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on Invoice [#INV-0001] for $[Amount], which is now [X] days past its [Date] due date. Per our agreement, a late fee of [1.5–2%] applies to balances unpaid after [15/30] days — as of today that brings the total to $[Amount + fee, if applicable].
>
> Could you let me know a specific date I can expect payment by? Happy to jump on a quick call if anything about the invoice is in question.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Email 3 — Final notice (~20-30 days overdue)

> Subject: Final notice — Invoice [#INV-0001] past due, action required
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> This is a final notice regarding Invoice [#INV-0001], now [X] days overdue. The total outstanding, including the late fee specified in our agreement, is $[Amount].
>
> Per Section [#] of our Service Agreement, I'll need to pause further work on [project/retainer] until this balance is settled. If payment isn't received by [firm date, e.g., 7 days out], I'll move this to [collections / a formal demand letter / next appropriate step].
>
> I'd much rather resolve this directly — please reach out if there's a reason for the delay I should know about, or send payment via [payment method/link] by the date above.
>
> [Your Name]

# Testimonial & Referral Request Scripts

**How to use:** Send at final delivery approval, before the final invoice is paid. Replace every [bracket] with specifics — the specificity is what makes these work.

---

## Script 1 — Testimonial request

> Subject: One quick favor before we wrap
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Really glad [specific outcome, e.g., "the new site launched on time and conversion is already up"]. Before we close things out, would you be willing to share a short testimonial I could use on my site?
>
> To make it easy, it'd help most if you spoke to [1-2 specific things, e.g., "how the process felt compared to what you expected, and the result on [metric]"]. A couple of sentences is perfect.
>
> No rush — [end of week] would be great if you have a moment.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Script 2 — Draft-it-for-them variant

*Use when the client agreed but hasn't written it, or is clearly short on time.*

> Hi [Name],
>
> I know your week is full, so I drafted something based on what you mentioned — feel free to edit, cut, or rewrite it however you like:
>
> *"[Draft testimonial in the client's voice — name the problem, what you did, and the specific result.]"*
>
> If that reads right to you, I'd love the OK to use it. Happy to change anything.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Script 3 — Referral request

> Hi [Name],
>
> It's been a pleasure working on [project]. If you know one or two other [specific role, e.g., "SaaS founders"] who are dealing with [specific problem you just solved], I'd really appreciate an introduction.
>
> Totally understand if nothing comes to mind — and if it's easier, I'm happy to write a short intro you could just forward.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your Name]

## Script 4 — Forwardable intro (for the referral)

*Send this to the client so all they do is forward it.*

> Hi [Referral Name],
>
> [Client Name] suggested we connect. I help [role] with [problem] — recently I [one-line result from the project]. If [likely pain point] is on your radar, I'd be glad to share how I approach it. No pressure either way.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]

## Script 5 — Light re-ask

*Use once, ~5-7 days after silence. Never more than once.*

> Hi [Name],
>
> Just floating this back up in case it slipped by — totally fine if now's not a good time. [One sentence: testimonial or intro ask restated.]
>
> Thanks either way,
> [Your Name]

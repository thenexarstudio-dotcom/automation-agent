## Before the call

- [ ] Look up the prospect's company/site and note anything specific to reference
- [ ] Know your own numbers cold (day rate, minimum project size) — see the [Freelance Day Rate Calculator]
- [ ] Have your calendar open in case you need to book a follow-up on the spot
- [ ] Block 30 minutes — don't run this call in a rushed 15-minute slot

## 1. Open (2 minutes)

> Hi [Name], thanks for making time. Before we dive in — my goal for this call is just to understand what you're dealing with and whether I'm the right fit to help. If it's not a fit, I'll tell you straight, and if it is, we'll figure out the right next step. Sound good?

## 2. Diagnose the real problem (8-10 minutes)

- "Walk me through what's prompting this — what's happening (or not happening) that made you start looking?"
- "What have you already tried, and what happened?"
- "If nothing changes, what does this cost you — in time, money, or missed opportunity?"
- "What would 'solved' actually look like six months from now?"

## 3. Qualify budget, authority, and timeline (8-10 minutes)

**Budget**
- "Do you have a budget range in mind, even a rough one?"
- *If they won't share a number:* "No worries — can you tell me what this problem is costing you every month it goes unsolved? That helps me size the right solution."

**Authority**
- "Aside from you, who else is involved in this decision?"
- "What does the approval process usually look like on your end?"

**Timeline**
- "Is there a date this needs to be solved by, or something driving urgency?"
- "What happens if this slips to next quarter instead?"

## 4. Close the call (5 minutes)

Choose the path that fits what you heard:

**A — Clear fit:**
> "Based on what you've shared, I think I can help. Here's what I'll do next: I'll put together a proposal and send it over by [date]. Before I do, I'll send a short onboarding questionnaire so I can make sure the proposal reflects exactly what you need."

**B — Possible fit, needs more information:**
> "This sounds like it could be a fit, but I want to make sure I scope it accurately. Can I send over a short questionnaire, and then we grab 15 minutes early next week to confirm before I send a proposal?"

**C — Not a fit:**
> "Based on what you've described, I don't think I'm the right fit for this — [one honest reason]. [If applicable: I'd recommend looking into [alternative type of provider/resource].] I appreciate you taking the time to talk it through."

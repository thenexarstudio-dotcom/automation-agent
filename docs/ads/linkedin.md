# LinkedIn Single-Image Ads — Consultant Client Ops

**How to use this:** Each concept is a Single Image Ad — intro text (two variants to A/B test), a headline (≤70 chars, verified below), and suggested audience targeting for LinkedIn's Campaign Manager. LinkedIn CPCs typically run several multiples above Meta or Google Search for this audience size, so budget accordingly and expect to spend more per click here than on the other two channels.

**Recommendation:** point LinkedIn traffic at the lead-magnet path (`/free-toolkit`), not the direct $39 checkout. At LinkedIn's cost-per-click, converting cold traffic straight to a purchase is an expensive ask; capturing the email first and nurturing toward the Kit gets more value out of the higher cost per click. If the direct path is tested here, do it deliberately as a small, separate experiment against the lead-magnet ad sets — not as the default.

**Do not launch:** wait until `/free-toolkit` (the lead-magnet landing page and email capture) is live, and the Gumroad checkout for the Kit is confirmed working, before activating these campaigns.

## Concept 1: "Free calculators, no signup"

**Intro text — variant A:**
How much should you actually charge? Four free browser calculators for freelancers and independent consultants — day rate, scope-creep cost, retainer profitability, and a scope of work generator. No signup required to use them.

**Intro text — variant B:**
Pricing yourself right is harder than it sounds. These four free calculators (day rate, scope-creep cost, retainer profitability, scope of work generator) run entirely in your browser — no account, no email wall. Grab the free client-ops cheatsheet while you're there.

**Headline:** Free calculators for freelance rates, retainers, and scope creep [64]

**Destination:** `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app/free-toolkit` (email capture → free cheatsheet → nurture toward the Kit)

**Audience targeting:**
- Job titles: Freelancer, Independent Consultant, Self-Employed, Consultant, Freelance Designer, Freelance Developer, Marketing Consultant
- Job function/seniority: Consulting; also target fractional roles — Fractional CMO, Fractional COO, Fractional CFO — who run project-based client engagements
- Skills: Freelancing, Consulting, Client Management, Project Management, Business Development
- Company size: 1 employee / self-employed (LinkedIn's smallest company-size bracket) to filter for solo operators

## Concept 2: "The client-ops kit for independent consultants"

**Intro text — variant A:**
Every client document from first proposal to final invoice, done. 20 fill-in-the-blank templates — proposals, contracts, scope of work, invoices, change orders, retainer agreements, NDAs — built specifically for independent consultants. One-time $39.

**Intro text — variant B:**
Stop rebuilding the same proposal and contract for every new client. The Consultant Client Ops Kit covers the entire engagement lifecycle in 20 editable documents (PDF + markdown), written for freelancers and consultants, not adapted from corporate boilerplate.

**Headline:** 20 client documents for independent consultants, from $39 [57]

**Destination (as a small, separate test against Concept 1):** `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app/templates` (direct → $39 Gumroad checkout)

**Audience targeting:**
- Job titles: Independent Consultant, Freelance Consultant, Self-Employed, Principal Consultant, Fractional Executive
- Job function/seniority: Consulting, Owner/Partner level for solo consultancies
- Skills: Management Consulting, Client Relationship Management, Contract Negotiation, Business Consulting
- Company size: 1 employee / self-employed

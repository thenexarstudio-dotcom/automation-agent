# Google Search Ads — Consultant Client Ops

**How to use this:** Copy each ad group's headlines and descriptions directly into a Google Ads Responsive Search Ad (RSA) — paste one headline/description per field, in the order listed. Character counts are shown in brackets and are verified against Google's limits (30 chars/headline, 90 chars/description); do not edit copy without re-counting. Keyword lists are starting points for exact/phrase match campaigns — add your own negative list on top of the shared negatives, and prune low-intent terms after the first 2–3 weeks of data. Landing pages referenced below assume the site is live at `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app`.

**Do not launch:** these campaigns should only go live once the Gumroad checkout for the Kit ($39) is connected and confirmed working, and (for the calculator ad group) the `/free-toolkit` lead-magnet page and email capture are live. Do not activate any ad group pointing at a page or checkout that isn't live yet.

---

## Shared negative keywords (add to all campaigns)

- `free` (broad, campaign-level negative — except the Calculator ad group, which legitimately sells "free")
- `jobs`
- `course` / `certification`
- `template` alone with `resume` or `cv` intent (e.g. "-resume template", "-cv template")

# Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook) — Consultant Client Ops

**How to use this:** Each concept below is a complete ad — creative direction, two lengths of primary text (use the short version for feed/Reels placements, the long version for placements with more room), a headline (≤40 chars, verified), a description, and a CTA button + destination URL. Build each concept as its own ad set so you can compare direct-to-checkout performance against lead-magnet performance. Targeting notes are starting points for Meta's detailed targeting search, not exhaustive audience definitions — expect to prune based on delivery data after the first week.

**Do not launch:** wait until the Gumroad checkout for the Kit is live and confirmed working, and until `/free-toolkit` (the lead-magnet landing page and email capture) is live, before activating the ad sets that point to it.

---

## Concept 1: "Stop rebuilding the same documents" (Direct → Kit)

**Creative/visual direction:** A clean flat-lay or screen-capture style graphic showing the 20 document titles fanned out or listed in a simple grid (Proposal, Contract, SOW, Invoice, NDA, etc.) with the price "$39" and "one-time" clearly visible — no stock photos of people, no fake UI.

**Primary text — short:**
Every client document from first proposal to final invoice — done. 20 fill-in-the-blank templates for freelancers and consultants. One-time $39, no subscription.

**Primary text — long:**
Stop rebuilding the same proposal, contract, and invoice for every new client. The Consultant Client Ops Kit is 20 professionally structured documents covering the whole client lifecycle — proposals, contracts, scope of work, change orders, invoices, retainer agreements, NDAs, a kill fee clause, and more. Built specifically for freelancers and independent consultants, not adapted from corporate boilerplate. PDF + editable markdown, one-time $39, use on unlimited client engagements.

**Headline:** 20 Client Docs for Freelancers [30]

**Description:** Proposals, contracts, invoices & more. PDF + markdown. $39 one-time.

**CTA button:** Shop Now → `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app/templates` (product/template listing, leads into $39 Gumroad checkout)

**Audience targeting suggestion:** Interests: freelancing, self-employment, independent consulting; software/tool interests like Bonsai, HoneyBook, Dubsado, AND.CO (client-management tool users are pre-qualified — they already feel this pain). Job titles/behaviors: freelancer, consultant, self-employed, small business owner. Narrow further to design, development, and marketing freelancers if budget allows, since they're the most document-heavy engagement types.

## Concept 2: "Get paid without the chase" (Direct → Kit, pain-point angle)

**Creative/visual direction:** Simple before/after style graphic or short screen-record: left side shows a messy DM/email thread negotiating scope and payment terms after the fact; right side shows a clean, structured invoice or change-order document from the Kit. **Primary text — short:**
"Just one more small thing" shouldn't be free. The Kit includes a change order template that turns scope creep into approved, billable work — plus 19 more documents for every stage of a client engagement.

Scope creep isn't a client problem, it's a documentation gap. The Consultant Client Ops Kit gives you a scope of work template with itemized deliverables and revision limits, a change order template to convert extra asks into billable work, a kill fee clause for cancellations, and late-payment reminder emails for the invoices that go quiet. 20 documents total, covering the entire client lifecycle from proposal to offboarding. Fill-in-the-blank, PDF + editable markdown, one-time $39.

**Headline:** Get Paid Without the Chase [26]

**Description:** Scope, change orders, invoices & kill fees. One-time $39.

**CTA button:** Shop Now → `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app/templates/scope-of-work-template` (direct to a specific template page → Gumroad checkout)

Behaviors: small business owners, self-employed. Consider a lookalike audience built from Kit purchasers once there's checkout data.

## Concept 3: "Free calculators, no signup" (Lead magnet → /free-toolkit)

**Creative/visual direction:** A simple screen-capture or mockup of the day rate calculator UI mid-use — an input field and a resulting day-rate number — framed as "try it right now," not a static brochure image. Keep it functional-looking, not glossy.

**Primary text — short:**
Free freelance calculators, no signup required: day rate, scope-creep cost, retainer profitability, and a scope of work generator. Try them in your browser right now.

**Primary text — long:**
How much should you actually charge? Four free browser calculators for freelancers and consultants — no signup, no email wall to use them: a day rate calculator that works from your real income target and billable capacity, a scope-creep cost calculator, a retainer profitability calculator, and a scope of work generator. Grab the free client-ops cheatsheet while you're there, and see the full 20-document Kit when you're ready to stop rebuilding paperwork for every client.

**Headline:** Free Freelance Rate Calculators [31]

**Description:** No signup. Day rate, retainer & scope-creep tools in your browser.

**CTA button:** Learn More → `https://consultant-client-ops.netlify.app/free-toolkit` (email capture → free cheatsheet → nurture toward the Kit)

**Audience targeting suggestion:** Interests: freelancing, gig economy, consulting, pricing strategy; broader top-of-funnel than Concepts 1–2 since this is a colder-intent, list-building play. Job titles: freelance designer, freelance developer, marketing consultant, freelance writer. Also test lookalikes off email-list subscribers once `/free-toolkit` has enough signups to build one.

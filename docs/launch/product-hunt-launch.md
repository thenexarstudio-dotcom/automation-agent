## Description (260 chars max)
Four free, no-signup calculators for freelancers — day rate, scope-creep cost, retainer profitability, and an SOW generator — plus 12 fill-in-the-blank client documents covering proposal to final invoice. Everything runs in your browser.

## First comment (maker comment — post immediately after launch)

Hey PH 👋

I built this after watching the same pattern kill freelance profitability over and over: good work, bad ops. Vague scopes that invite creep, hourly rates picked by vibes, proposals that die in inboxes.

**The free stuff (no signup, runs entirely in your browser):**
- Day Rate Calculator — your real rate from income target + honest billable days
- Scope Creep Cost Calculator — what "just one more thing" actually costs per year
- Retainer Profitability Calculator — is that retainer offer actually above your target rate?
- SOW Generator — a clean scope-of-work draft in four fields

**The paid stuff ($39, one-time):** 16 client documents — proposal, SOW, contract, change order, invoice, retainer, and more — as PDFs + markdown that imports straight into Notion.

Nothing here needs an account or sends your data anywhere; the calculators are fully client-side.

Would love to hear what ops documents you keep rebuilding from scratch — that's the roadmap.

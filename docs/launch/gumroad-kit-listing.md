## Description

**Stop rebuilding the same documents for every client.**

The Consultant Client Ops Kit is 16 professionally structured documents covering the entire client lifecycle — written specifically for freelancers and independent consultants, not adapted from corporate boilerplate.

**What's inside:**

1. Consulting Proposal Template — the seven-section structure that closes
2. Scope of Work Template — itemized deliverables, exclusions, and revision limits that stop scope creep before it starts
3. Service Agreement Template — payment terms, IP transfer on final payment, termination, and liability, in plain language
4. Client Onboarding Questionnaire — every logistics question, asked once, up front
5. Change Order Template — turn "just one more small thing" into an approved, billable addition
6. Invoice Template — formatted to clear accounts payable on the first pass
7. Client Offboarding Checklist — the referral-and-testimonial engine most freelancers skip
8. Rate Card / Pricing Sheet Template — three tiers that raise your average deal size
9. Retainer Agreement Template — convert good clients into recurring revenue
10. Project Kickoff Checklist — a week-one that actually moves
11. Testimonial & Referral Request Scripts — word-for-word asks that get answered
12. Weekly Client Update Template — pre-empt every "how's it going?" email
13. Discovery Call Script — qualify a lead before you waste a proposal on it
14. Project Completion Report — make your results undeniable at handoff
15. Freelance NDA Template — a mutual NDA for sensitive engagements
16. Late Payment Reminder Emails — three escalating, ready-to-send drafts

**Formats:** print-ready PDFs + editable markdown files that import natively into Notion (Import → Text & Markdown) and Google Docs. A START-HERE guide walks you through setup in minutes.

**One-time purchase. No subscription.** Use them on unlimited client engagements of your own.

*The legal templates are structured starting points reflecting common practice, not legal advice — have a lawyer adapt them to your jurisdiction for high-value engagements.*
